# Viah

A structured travel-planning app: pick a city, drop an anchor, choose your moods — and get a walkable, day-by-day itinerary of real, quality-checked places, in a second. No hallucinated venues.

**▶ Live demo:** https://jaco1103.github.io/viah-showcase/

## How it's different

Most travel AI predicts plausible-sounding text and invents places. Viah does the opposite: every place is real — sourced and quality-gated ahead of time — and your itinerary is built by a deterministic algorithm, not a live AI call. Fast, reliable, and the same every time.

## Built with

iOS (SwiftUI · MapKit) · Supabase (PostgreSQL · PostGIS) · enriched once with Claude · places from Google, geo from OpenStreetMap.
